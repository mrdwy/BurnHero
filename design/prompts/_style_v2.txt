Mobile app UI screen mockup for an Android phone (portrait, 9:16), 16-bit pixel art style like a retro RPG game menu. Crisp hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur, chunky 2px pixel borders on every panel, flat colors. Strict limited palette: deep night background #1B1F3B, panel #2B2F5A, panel border #0E1028, cream text #F4EBD0, health red #E8505B, stamina green #5BD16B, mana/water blue #4FA3E0, gold coins #F5C542, XP purple #9B6CFF, orange accent #FF8A3D. Pixel bitmap font for all text, Chinese labels rendered clearly. Clean, readable, game-like but usable as a real app, generous spacing, bottom navigation bar with 5 slots: 任务 (sword), 角色 (helmet), a larger raised orange center button with a big pixel "+" labeled 记录, 奖励 (chest), 计划 (scroll). Status bar at top.
